# Platform Engineering Perú

Connecting people, knowledge, and technology to advance Cloud Native practices and developer experience in Peru.

We bring together platform engineers, DevOps practitioners, SREs, developers, cloud specialists, and architects to share practical experience through talks, workshops, and community gatherings.

Our identity combines three layers: developer experience, platform orchestration, and infrastructure. Ukuku, our spectacled-bear mascot, represents curiosity, care, and shared paths forward.

## Brand kit

Use the supplied artwork and consult [Brand Guidelines](BRAND_GUIDELINES.md) for palette values, file organization, and usage principles. Both light and dark logo treatments are included. Keep original proportions and choose a variant that contrasts with its background.
